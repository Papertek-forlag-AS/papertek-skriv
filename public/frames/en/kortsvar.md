# Short answer

## Metadata
genre: kortsvar
level: vgs
description: A short comparative task - typically 250 words about two texts

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Present both texts briefly: title, author, genre and year of publication. Get straight to the point — you only have about 250 words. State what you are going to compare.
- Text 1 is... by... and text 2 is... by...
- I will compare these texts with a focus on...

## Comparison
spinner: sammenligning
> What do the texts have in common? Point out similarities in theme, devices, perspective or form. Use short quotations or concrete examples from the texts as evidence.
- Both texts deal with..., but in different ways...
- A clear similarity is that both...

## Differences
spinner: forskjeller
> What sets the texts apart? Be precise and concrete. Show the differences with examples from both texts. Every sentence should serve the comparison — do not retell the plot.
- The most important difference is that text 1..., while text 2...
- This is expressed in the way that...

## Summary
spinner: oppsummering
> End with one or two sentences that draw the threads together. What does the comparison show? Do not introduce new points here.
- The comparison shows that...
