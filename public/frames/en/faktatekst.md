# Factual text

## Metadata
genre: faktatekst
level: barneskole
description: A factual text that explains a topic, with its own paragraph for each subtopic

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Tell the reader what the text is about. Write one or two sentences that make the reader curious about the topic.
- This text is about...
- Did you know that...

## Facts
spinner: fakta

### Subtopic 1
> Write about one thing at a time. Start the paragraph with a sentence that says what it is about, and explain with facts you know or have found.
- One important thing to know is that...
- Scientists have found that...

### Subtopic 2
> A new subtopic means a new paragraph. Use subject words when you can, and explain them if they are difficult.
- Another exciting thing is that...
- This means that...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> Finish by summing up the most important points, or tell the reader why the topic matters or why it is exciting.
- Now you have learned that...
- The most important thing to remember is...
