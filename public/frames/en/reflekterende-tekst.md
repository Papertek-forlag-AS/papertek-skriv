# Reflective essay

## Metadata
genre: reflekterende-tekst
level: vgs
description: An exploratory, personal text where you think your way through a topic

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> What are you going to reflect on, and why? Present the topic and explain why it interests you. A reflective essay often starts with a sense of wonder, an experience, or a question you do not yet have the answer to.
- I have long wondered why...
- What got me thinking about this was...

## Exploration
spinner: utforsking
> Explore the topic from several sides. You do not need to conclude — try instead to see the matter from different angles. Ask questions along the way. It is your thinking process that should be visible to the reader.
- On the one hand, you could think that...
- But what if it is really about...
- A question that arises is...

## Personal experience
spinner: refleksjon
> Connect your reflection to something you have experienced, observed or felt yourself. Personal experiences make the text concrete and credible. Show how the experience links to the wider topic.
- I remember one time...
- This experience made me realise that...

## New perspectives
spinner: refleksjon
> What have you come to understand while writing? Has your view changed? Have you discovered something you had not thought of before? Uncertainty is perfectly fine — it shows honest thinking.
- What I had not considered before is...
- Perhaps it is the case that...
- I am still unsure about..., but I think that...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> Where does the reflection land? You do not need a clear answer — a reflective essay can end with a new question, an insight, or the realisation that the topic is more complex than you thought.
- What this reflection has shown me is that...
- I still do not quite know..., but I now understand that...
