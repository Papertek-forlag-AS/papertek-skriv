# Formal letter

## Metadata
genre: formelt-brev
level: ungdomsskole
description: A formal letter or email to a recipient you do not know - complaint, enquiry or notification

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Say who you are (if needed) and why you are writing. The recipient should understand your errand after the first paragraph. Use polite, neutral language — no slang.
- I am writing to you because...
- With reference to..., I would like to...

## The matter
spinner: sak
> Explain the matter properly: What happened? When? Where? Stick to the facts and stay objective, even if you are dissatisfied. Include the details the recipient needs (dates, order numbers, places).
- On..., I experienced that...
- The matter concerns...
- I would particularly like to point out that...

## What I am asking for
spinner: handling
> State clearly what you want the recipient to do: answer a question, put something right, give a refund, change something. One clear request is easier to answer than many vague ones.
- I therefore ask that...
- I would appreciate a response regarding...
- A good outcome for me would be that...

## Closing
spinner: avslutning
> Close politely, thank the recipient for their help, and say how you can be contacted. Remember "Yours sincerely" and your full name.
- Thank you in advance for your help.
- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Yours sincerely
