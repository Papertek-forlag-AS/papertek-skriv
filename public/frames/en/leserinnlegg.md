description: A short, punchy opinion text reacting to a current issue

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Say clearly what you are reacting to and what you think. A letter to the editor is short and direct — get straight to the point.
- I strongly object to...
- It is about time someone spoke up about...

## Main body
spinner: argument

### Main argument
> Present your most important argument. Be clear and concrete — why do you think what you think?
- The most important point is that...
- The reason this is so serious is that...

### Supporting argument
> Build on your case with an extra argument or example that strengthens it.
- In addition, we can see that...
- A good example of this is...

### Counter-argument and response
> Show that you understand the other side, but explain why you still stand by your opinion.
- Some may argue that...
- But that does not hold up, because...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> End with a clear appeal or demand. What do you want readers or decision-makers to do?
- That is why I demand that...
- It is high time we...
