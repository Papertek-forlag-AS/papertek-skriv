# Opinion piece

## Metadata
genre: kronikk
level: vgs
description: An argumentative opinion piece with a clear stance on a current issue

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Open with a topical hook that catches the reader's attention. Present the issue and your stance on it.
- It was recently revealed that... This shows that...
- In this piece I will argue that...

## Main body
spinner: argument
> Build your argumentation with clear arguments. Each argument should contain a claim, a reason and an example.

### Argument 1
> Present your strongest argument. What do you think, and why?
- The most important argument is that...
- This can be justified by...

### Argument 2
> Present a supporting argument that builds on the first one.
- In addition, it is important to point out that...
- A good example of this is...

### Counter-argument and response
> Show that you see the issue from more than one side. Present a counter-argument and explain why you still hold your view.
- Some might argue that...
- Nevertheless, I believe that..., because...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> Sum up your message and end with a strong appeal or conclusion that gives the reader something to think about.
- All in all, this shows that...
- That is why I believe we must...
