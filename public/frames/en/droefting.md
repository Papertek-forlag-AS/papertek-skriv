# Discussion essay

## Metadata
genre: droefting
level: vgs
description: A structured discussion essay with arguments for and against

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Present the topic and the question you are going to discuss. Give the reader background information and explain why this is an important issue.
- Start by describing the topic and why it matters right now...
- The question I am going to discuss is...

## Argument for
spinner: argument
> Present the first argument supporting one side of the issue. Use the three-step method: make a claim, back it up with a source or example, and explain the connection.

### Claim
spinner: argument
> Formulate a clear claim that supports one side.
- One important argument in favour of this is that...

### Support
spinner: eksempel
> Back up the claim with facts, research, examples or references.
- This is supported by...

### Explain
spinner: overgang
> Explain why this argument is relevant to the question.
- This means that...

## Argument against
spinner: motargument
> Present an argument from the other side of the issue. Use the three-step method here too, so the discussion is thorough.

### Claim
spinner: motargument
> Formulate a clear claim that represents the opposite point of view.
- On the other hand, it could be argued that...

### Support
spinner: eksempel
> Back up this claim with facts, research or examples.
- This is backed up by...

### Explain
spinner: overgang
> Explain why this counter-argument is important to consider.
- The consequence of this is that...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> Sum up the discussion and present your own conclusion. What do you think after weighing both sides?
- Having discussed both sides, I believe that...
- The conclusion is that...
