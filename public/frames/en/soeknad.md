# Job application

## Metadata
genre: soeknad
level: ungdomsskole
description: A job application or cover letter - convince the recipient that you are the right person

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Say clearly what you are applying for and where you saw the advertisement. Get to the point quickly — the recipient reads many applications.
- I am writing to apply for the position of...
- With reference to the advertisement on..., I would like to apply for...

## Why me
spinner: kvalifikasjoner
> Describe what you can do and what relevant experience you have: work experience, positions of responsibility, school subjects, spare-time activities. Show with examples rather than just making claims.
- I have experience with... from...
- Through..., I have learned to...
- As..., I was responsible for...

## Motivation
spinner: motivasjon
> Why do you want this particular job or place? Show that you know something about where you are applying, and what you can contribute.
- I would like this position because...
- What appeals to me about... is...
- I can contribute...

## Closing
spinner: avslutning
> Close politely and briefly. Say that you would be happy to come to an interview, and when you can start. Remember "Yours sincerely" and your full name.
- I would be happy to attend an interview and can start...
- Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.
- Yours sincerely
