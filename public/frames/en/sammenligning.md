# Comparative essay

## Metadata
genre: sammenligning
level: vgs
description: An in-depth comparative analysis of two or more texts

## Introduction
spinner: innledning
> Present the texts you are going to compare: title, author, genre and context. State clearly what you are comparing (theme, form, perspective) and why the comparison is interesting.
- I am going to compare... by... with... by...
- The focus of the comparison is... because...

## Similarities
spinner: likheter
> What do the texts have in common? Organise by point, not text by text. Each paragraph should discuss both texts. Use concrete examples and short quotations as evidence.
- A clear common feature is that both texts...
- Text 1 shows this through..., while text 2 does it through...
- This similarity can be explained by the fact that...

## Differences
spinner: forskjeller
> How do the texts differ from each other? Be specific — point to concrete places in the texts. Show not only that they differ, but how and why the differences arise.
- The most important difference concerns...
- In text 1 this is expressed in the way that..., while text 2...
- One possible explanation for this difference is...

## Connections
spinner: forskjeller
> Put the similarities and differences into context. Why do they matter? What do they tell us about the genre, the period, or the theme? Here you can bring in context that sheds light on the comparison.
- These differences can be seen in the light of...
- The comparison shows that..., which can be explained by...

## Conclusion
spinner: avslutning
> What does the comparison reveal? Sum up the most important findings and make a judgement: which text is more successful at what you have compared? Justify your answer.
- The comparison reveals that...
- Overall, text... is the most effective because...
