## Voice and perspective
spinner: skildring
> Who is speaking in the text? Is the voice close or distant, ironic or sincere, poetic or conversational? Be consistent - or break the pattern deliberately. Perspective is a choice that shapes the whole text.
- (Commit to the voice you have chosen - let it shape word choice, rhythm, sentence structure...)

## Ending
spinner: avslutning
> Land the text. The ending can mirror the opening, surprise, leave an open question, or offer a final image that lingers. A good ending changes how the reader understands everything that came before.
- (Write an ending that gives the text resonance - something that stays with the reader...)
